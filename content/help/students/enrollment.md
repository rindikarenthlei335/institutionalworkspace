# Student Enrollment & Management / Zirlai Lakluh leh Enkawl Dan

## English (en)
### How to Enroll Students
To add students to your school, you can either:
1. **Individual Admission**: Go to **Admin -> Students & Guardians** and click **New Admission**. Enter the student's legal name, gender, date of birth, class, and guardian phone number.
2. **Bulk Excel Import**: Go to **Admin -> Data Hub -> Import Center**, select **Students**, download the official Excel template (`.xlsx`), fill in the student rows, and upload. The system performs dry-run validations, detects duplicates based on admission number, and imports in transactional batches.

## Mizo (lus)
### Zirlai Lakluh Dan
Sikula zirlai thar thun luh nan kawng hnih a awm:
1. **Luh Dilna Mal Te Te**: **Admin -> Students & Guardians** ah kal la, **New Admission** hmet rawh. Zirlai hming, mipa/hmeichhia, pianni, pawl, leh nu-leh-pa biakpawhna dah rawh.
2. **Excel hmanga a rualin thun**: **Admin -> Data Hub -> Import Center** ah kal la, **Students** thlangin Excel template (`.xlsx`) download rawh. Row-ah zirlai chanchin dah la, upload leh rawh. System-in duplicate a enfiah hnuah batch fel fai takin a thun lut ang.
