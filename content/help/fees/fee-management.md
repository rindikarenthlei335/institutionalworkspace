# Fee Management & Defaulters / Fee Khawn leh Ba En Dan

## English (en)
### How to Manage Fees & Collect Payments
1. **Fee Structures**: Define tuition, admission, exam, and hostel fee line items per class.
2. **Collect Payments**: Go to **Admin -> Fees & Receipts**. You can record cash/offline payments with an auto-generated official numbered receipt (e.g. `REC-2024-001`).
3. **Defaulter Tracking**: The Defaulters tab lists all students with unpaid balances past the due date. You can filter by class and send payment reminders.

## Mizo (lus)
### Fee Enkawl leh Khawn Dan
1. **Fee Ruahmanna**: Pawl tin tan zirna fee, admission fee, exam fee, leh hostel fee ruahman rawh.
2. **Pawisa Dawnsawn**: **Admin -> Fees & Receipts** ah kal la. Pawisa fai lut receipt nambar fel tak (e.g. `REC-2024-001`) pe chhuak nghal rawh.
3. **Fee Ba Enna**: Defaulters tab-ah fee la pe lo zirlai zawng zawng an hming a lang ang a, hriattirna thawn theih a ni.
