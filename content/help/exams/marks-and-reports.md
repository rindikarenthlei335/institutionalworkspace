# Exams, Marks Entry & Report Cards / Exam, Marks leh Marksheet

## English (en)
### How to Enter Marks and Publish Report Cards
1. **Setup**: In **Admin -> Exams & Marksheets**, configure academic terms (Unit Test, Mid Term, Final) and max/pass marks.
2. **Marks Entry**: Use the high-speed keyboard grid or Excel upload to input subject marks.
3. **Publishing**: Click **Publish** to freeze marks snapshots, calculate ranks and percentages, and generate printable A4/CR80 marksheets with tamper-proof QR verification codes.
4. **Parent Visibility**: Published results immediately appear in parent and student portals and the mobile app.

## Mizo (lus)
### Marks Chhutluh leh Marksheet Tichhuah Dan
1. **Ruahmanna**: **Admin -> Exams & Marksheets** ah exam term leh subject marks sang ber/tling zat ruahman rawh.
2. **Marks Chhutluh**: Spreadsheet grid emaw Excel hmangin marks thun lut zung zung rawh.
3. **Publishing**: **Publish** hmet la, rank leh percentage chhutin A4 marksheet leh QR code rintlak a inpeih nghal ang.
4. **Nu leh Pa Hmuhtirna**: Result tichhuah tawh chu zirlai leh nu-leh-pa app leh portal-ah a lang nghal ang.
