# Custom Module Builder / No-Code Module Siamna

## English (en)
### How to Build Custom No-Code Modules
1. **Templates**: In **Admin -> Custom Module Builder**, choose from 6 turnkey templates (Library, Transport, Homework, Events, Visitors, Inventory) or build from scratch.
2. **Schema & Fields**: Add fields with types such as text, number, currency, date, single-select, multiselect, and relational lookups to students or staff.
3. **Data Records**: Records are saved in high-performance schemaless JSONB with index support and instantly integrated into system navigation.

## Mizo (lus)
### No-Code Module Siam leh Enkawl Dan
1. **Module Template-te**: **Admin -> Custom Module Builder** ah template 6 (Lehkhabu, Bus, Homework, Events, Visitors, Hmanraw Enkawlna) atangin a thlan theih.
2. **Field Dahna**: Field hrang hrang (thu, nambar, pawisa, ni, thlan theih, zirlai thlunzawm) dah theih a ni.
3. **Data Vawnna**: Data JSONB hmanga vawn that a ni a, navigation-ah a lang nghal ang.
