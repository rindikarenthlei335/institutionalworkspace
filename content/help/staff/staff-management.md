# Staff & Faculty Management / Zirtirtu leh Staff Enkawl Dan

## English (en)
### How to Manage Staff and Faculty Roster
1. **Staff Profiles**: Go to **Admin -> Staff & Teachers** to maintain employee ID, designations, teaching qualifications, and contact details.
2. **Website Synchronization**: Toggle the "Show on Website" switch on any staff profile to automatically publish their bio and photo to the public website faculty roster without double data entry.
