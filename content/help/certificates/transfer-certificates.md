# Transfer & Bonafide Certificates / Sikul Insaun Hriatpuina (TC)

## English (en)
### How to Issue Official Transfer & Bonafide Certificates
1. **Issuance**: In **Admin -> Certificates**, select the student from Data Hub. Student details, admission date, class, and conduct are automatically merged into the certificate.
2. **Sequential Numbering**: Each issued certificate is stamped with an official sequential identifier (e.g., `TC-MC-2024-001`) and a public verification QR code.
3. **Public Verification**: Third parties or other institutions can scan the QR code to verify validity at `/verify/certificate/[token]` without exposing private student records.

## Mizo (lus)
### Transfer Certificate (TC) leh Bonafide Pekchhuah Dan
1. **Pekchhuahna**: **Admin -> Certificates** ah zirlai thlang rawh. Admission kum, pawl, leh nungchang an inziak nghal vek ang.
2. **Serial Number**: Certificate tinah serial number felfai tak (e.g. `TC-MC-2024-001`) leh QR code dah nghal zel a ni.
3. **QR Endikna**: Pawl dang emaw sikul dangin QR code scan hmangin `/verify/certificate/[token]` ah certificate dik zia an enfiah thei ang.
